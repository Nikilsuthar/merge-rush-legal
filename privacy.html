Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Merge Rush 2048 (the “App”), an Android puzzle game published by Nikil Suthar (“we”, “us”), handles information. We designed the App to need as little data as possible.

1. The short version

  • There is no account, no login, and no sign-up.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location.
  • We do not run our own servers that store information about you.
  • The App shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may use your device’s advertising ID and similar technical data (see section 3).

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following only on your device, using Android’s local storage, so the game can remember it next time:

  • Best scores for each grid size
  • Which levels you have completed
  • Your settings (sound, vibration, animated background, swipe sensitivity)

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data. You can also reset it in Settings.

3. Third-party services: Google AdMob

The App is free and supported by advertising. It uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show banner, interstitial (full-screen) and rewarded (opt-in video) ads. Google may collect and use data such as:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device model, OS version, language, app versionServing and measuring ads, preventing fraud and abuse, analytics
Ad interactions (impressions, taps, video views)Ad delivery, reporting, rewards for watching rewarded ads

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive this data and we do not sell it.

4. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and similar regions): where required by law, the App shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads. You can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown when applicable).
  • Personalised ads: on Android you can opt out or reset your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path varies by device).
  • Delete local data: uninstall the App, clear its storage in Android settings, or use the reset options inside the App.

5. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We configure ads to a family-friendly maximum content rating. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help.

6. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: needed to load and show ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ads SDK as described above.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or location.

7. Data sharing, sale and retention

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is shared only with Google through the ads SDK as described above. Because we do not collect personal data on our own servers, we have nothing to retain, export or delete on your behalf other than what is stored locally on your device.

8. Security

Local data stays inside the App’s private storage area on your device. Network communication by the ads SDK uses encrypted connections where available.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or India’s DPDP Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. As we hold no personal data about you, requests about ad-related data are best directed to Google via your Google Account controls. You may also contact us with any question.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page, and the effective date above will change. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at https://github.com/Nikilsuthar/merge-rush-legal/issues.
